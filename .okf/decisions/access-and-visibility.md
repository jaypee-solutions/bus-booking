# Access and visibility

Bookers see their own retained details and other bookings' interval and Nutzergruppe only;
administrators see all retained details. The anonymous plan follows the same minimal disclosure,
and administration uses named personal Authentik accounts rather than shared or legacy accounts.
See `docs/PRD.md` and `docs/adr/0001-public-plan-privacy.md`.
